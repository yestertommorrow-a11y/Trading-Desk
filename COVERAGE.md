# Market Chameleon workflow coverage

Reference reviewed on 15 November 2026. The public [Market Chameleon homepage](https://marketchameleon.com/), [site map](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/SiteFeatures), [AAPL summary](https://marketchameleon.com/Overview/AAPL/), [SPY summary](https://marketchameleon.com/Overview/SPY/), and [option chain guide](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/OptionChain) establish the workflows below. OpenBB supplies the new terminal's market data through its configured providers, with fields and timestamps reported per dataset. A provider may cover the underlying while lacking its listed options, company filings, earnings, or ETF portfolio data. [OpenBB chain specification](https://docs.openbb.co/odp/python/reference/derivatives/options/chains/)

## How the reference works

- The homepage combines analyst entry points with market monitoring. Global symbol search opens a dedicated research page. Global reports aggregate across symbols and lead back to their research pages. The landing page groups alerts, most active options, largest prints, expiry-day activity, educational content, trade ideas, watchlists and stock movers. [Homepage](https://marketchameleon.com/)
- A company summary brings together price and volume, a historical chart, implied volatility, option activity, earnings, dividends, news, company links, and fundamentals. The stock detail menu also exposes historical prices, insider transactions, statement detail, peer comparisons, valuation, and event studies. An ETF keeps the trading and options views and adds fund profile and holdings. [AAPL summary](https://marketchameleon.com/Overview/AAPL/), [SPY summary](https://marketchameleon.com/Overview/SPY/)
- The chain aligns calls and puts around a shared strike, with a separate view for each expiry. Contracts expose bid and ask, implied volatility, volume, open interest, and Greeks. The guide describes expiry markers for known company events, alternative theoretical marks, a contract drilldown, and historical CSV downloads for entitled users. [Option chain guide](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/OptionChain), [AAPL chain](https://marketchameleon.com/Overview/AAPL/OptionChain/)
- Options reports compare market and sector ETFs, screen across expirations, rank implied volatility, detect spikes in option volume, and analyze open-interest changes. Trade screeners use individual prints to report unusual trades, complex spreads and quote-side classifications. Reference data and access differ by report and subscription. [Market snapshot](https://marketchameleon.com/Reports/MarketSnapshot), [unusual option activity](https://marketchameleon.com/Reports/UnusualOptionVolumeReport), [open-interest dashboard](https://marketchameleon.com/Reports/OpenInterestDashboard/), [block trade guide](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/option-block-trades)
- The earnings tools place current option prices alongside the historical response to reports. Their inputs include whether the report is confirmed, the release time, the first expiration covering the event, historical price gaps and close-to-close moves, and changes in historical straddle marks and IV. [Earnings methodology](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/Earnings)
- The reference separates current strategy payoff, screening for combinations, and trades simulated on historical options prices. Its menu spans single legs, verticals, calendars, butterflies, condors, straddles, conversions and reversals. The backtester describes recurring entries and catalyst-driven trades. [Site map](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/SiteFeatures), [options backtester](https://marketchameleon.com/Options/Backtester)

## Terminal coverage and data dependencies

| Research workflow | Terminal scope | Data needed for wider parity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock and ETF lookup | Search a symbol and open its research view where the provider resolves it. Display supported company or fund identity, quote, and price history. | Broader international instruments, exchange aliases, delisted names, and exhaustive cross-listings require suitable reference data. |
| Equity research | Show supported company profile, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, financial ratios and filing links. Review dividends, historical earnings, available calendar reports and news. | Detailed analyst estimates and revisions, peer multiples, company valuation, guidance, insider transactions and point-in-time financial datasets require additional integrations. |
| ETF research | Show available fund information, holdings, distributions and news. Nasdaq supplies up to ten positions; SEC N-PORT can provide a full portfolio as of its disclosure date, as verified for SPY. | Daily positions, exposure histories, daily flows, premium or discount to NAV and universe-wide fund screening require expanded fund datasets. [ETF screener](https://marketchameleon.com/Screeners/ETFs) |
| Option chain | Filter a selected symbol's chain by expiration and contract attributes. Compare calls and puts by strike, view provided quotes, Greeks, IV, volume and OI, and export current results to CSV. | Session replay, expiry catalyst overlays, contract history, changes against the prior close, reference theoretical marks and historical CSVs require matching time series and event data. |
| Volatility | Inspect snapshot IV across expirations and strikes, interpolate 25-delta skew, and estimate realized volatility from price history. Calculate 30-day IV range rank when the quote supplies current IV and the annual high/low. | Constant-maturity IV history, percentiles, historical skew, volatility seasonality, risk-premium history, IV movers and calibrated surfaces need a consistent historical series and agreed convention. [IV rankings](https://marketchameleon.com/volreports/volatilityrankings) |
| Activity or screening | Compare snapshot contract liquidity, volume and OI within the selected instrument. | True relative option volume requires like-for-like historical session totals. Day-over-day OI requires two official OI snapshots. Cross-market screens require a timestamp-aligned, multi-symbol dataset. |
| Trades and flow | A current chain can show aggregate volumes and OI per contract. | Time and sales, blocks, multi-leg linkage, buyer or seller classification, opening or closing inference and trade condition filters require entitled trade prints and aligned quotes. |
| Earnings and catalysts | Review event and earnings information where published by the configured provider. | Earnings IV crush, historical implied-versus-realized moves, historical straddle performance and post-earnings drift studies need point-in-time options, release timestamps and a trading calendar. Biotech, M&A, corporate actions and event scans require specialized data. |
| Strategy analysis | Model expiration payoff for user-selected same-expiry positions, with quotes or explicitly entered premiums and visible assumptions. | Calendar pricing, calibrated pre-expiry marks, historical return distributions, win rates and execution-aware backtests require more market data and explicit modeling assumptions. |
| Market monitoring | Use a watchlist to revisit selected symbols. | Full market dashboards, premarket and after-hours rankings, stock movers and volume bursts, auction imbalance, short-sale threshold lists, industry performance, calendar alerts, stock seasonality, news scanners, notifications and synchronized accounts require more feeds or services. [Site map](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/SiteFeatures) |

## Interpret the data

- Compare fields using their own provider and market time. A retrieval time records when the application received a response. Use a quote time or session date when the upstream feed supplies one. A valid symbol can return an incomplete or empty chain. [OpenBB chain specification](https://docs.openbb.co/odp/python/reference/derivatives/options/chains/)
- Volume counts trades in contracts. OI measures outstanding contracts and can refer to the prior completed session. A put/call ratio should name its numerator, denominator, and scope. A positive change in aggregate volume or OI alone cannot identify trade direction, aggressor, opening or closing status, or dealer positioning. [Market Chameleon OI guide](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/open-interest-analysis), [block trade guide](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/option-block-trades)
- The strike nearest the spot is a transparent ATM proxy. A chain's per-expiry ATM IV is distinct from a constant 30-day IV series. The ATM straddle price divided by spot is a straddle breakeven proxy. Using annualized IV times the square root of the expiry fraction gives a different expected-move proxy. Use the label for the calculation shown. [Market Chameleon volatility guide](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/Volatility), [earnings methodology](https://marketchameleon.com/Learn/Earnings)
- Market Chameleon reports the 30-day IV percentile as the fraction of its historical daily readings below the current value. It separately reports the current value's position between the historical minimum and maximum. Both require the underlying IV history. [IV rankings](https://marketchameleon.com/volreports/volatilityrankings)
- Bid and ask are quoted prices. Their midpoint and a prior last trade are indicative marks with different timing and liquidity. Compare contract multipliers before aggregating P&L and pay attention to adjusted contracts or nonstandard deliverables. [OpenBB chain specification](https://docs.openbb.co/odp/python/reference/derivatives/options/chains/)

## Evidence and verification limits

The public search index and Market Chameleon guides establish the exposed navigation, described interactions and published methodologies. Authenticated workflows and production data entitlements are outside this verification. REA 6.9.0 ran from a workspace-local CLI and validated the public homepage, AAPL overview, AAPL option chain and SPY overview scenario. Chrome and Chromium each exited during browser startup in the managed Windows shell. A separate direct request returned an access-denied page. The report therefore uses the available public sources and has no REA browser capture. [REA browser scenario guide](https://github.com/morluto/rea/blob/main/docs/guides/web/browser-scenario-contract.md)
